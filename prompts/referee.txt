You sort a player's move in a role-playing game. Answer with the form only.
kind: "talk" if the player speaks to or asks something of a character; "look" if the player searches, examines, studies, listens or otherwise tries to learn something; "do" for any other action.
who: the character spoken to, or "nobody".
